Senior analyst scope

Depth across the full decision chain.

The work goes beyond dashboards: it defines reliable inputs, measures uncertainty, documents tradeoffs, and connects findings to operating decisions.

01 / PROBLEM FRAMING

Decision architecture

Stakeholder question, decision owner, metric tree, analytical grain, thresholds, and what would change the recommendation.

02 / DATA ENGINEERING

Reliable analytical layers

Schema normalization, typed fields, deduplication, conformed dimensions, reusable marts, and automated quality assertions.

03 / ANALYTICAL METHODS

Evidence with context

Time-series decomposition, Pareto concentration, segmentation, correlation, benchmark design, anomaly flags, and forecast backtesting.

04 / DATA PRODUCT

Governed metrics & communication

Definitions, lineage, freshness, limitations, executive summaries, drill paths, and specific actions tied to the evidence.

Working method

Five gates before a number becomes a decision.

This is the operating discipline used across every project in the portfolio.

01

Frame

Define the decision, audience, unit of analysis, and success metric.

02

Profile

Test uniqueness, completeness, validity, consistency, and freshness.

03

Model

Create reusable dimensions, facts, metric logic, and documented lineage.

04

Challenge

Check alternative explanations, sensitivity, bias, and forecast error.

05

Decide

State the action, expected benefit, risk, owner, and next measurement.
