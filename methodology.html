Methods, governance & reproducibility

The portfolio is designed as an auditable analytical product: source files are immutable, transformations are reproducible, metrics are defined, and limitations travel with the result.

Data quality framework

Six dimensions with explicit tests

DimensionQuestionPortfolio control
UniquenessIs the declared key unique?Complaint ID, Facility ID, series + month
CompletenessAre required values present?Coverage rates reported before averages
ValidityDo types and ranges make sense?Date parsing, rating bounds, nonnegative counts
ConsistencyDo related fields agree?High ratings checked against worse-measure counts
ScopeDoes the extract match the decision?Arizona / 2024 assertions for CFPB data
FreshnessWhat data vintage is represented?Agency release dates and latest month retained

Metric contracts

A KPI is a definition plus a denominator

NAMETimely-response rate

Complaints marked “Yes” divided by complaints with a response-status record.

GRAINComplaint

One row per Complaint ID after scope and validity controls.

LIMITProcess, not outcome

Does not measure satisfaction, fairness, or resolution quality.

NAMERating coverage

Facilities with numeric overall ratings divided by all listed facilities.

GRAINFacility

One row per CMS Facility ID in the released national file.

LIMITMissing not neutral

Unrated hospitals remain a separate coverage-review population.

Analytical methods

Methods chosen for the decision—not for novelty

Pareto concentration

Identifies which entities explain most volume and therefore deserve focused controls.

Time-series indexing

Compares series with different units by normalizing them to a common baseline.

Spearman correlation

Measures monotonic association without assuming linearity or normal distributions.

Forecast backtesting

Compares candidate methods on an unseen holdout window before selecting a baseline.

Rule-based screening

Creates explainable review queues when a black-box score would reduce trust.

Sensitivity & caveats

Documents missingness, denominator choices, revisions, and alternate explanations.

Modeling pattern

Separate dimensions, facts, and semantic metrics

-- Complaint mart: stable grain = one complaint
CREATE VIEW mart_complaint_performance AS
SELECT
  c.complaint_id,
  d.calendar_month,
  p.product_group,
  co.company_name,
  c.state_code,
  c.timely_response_flag,
  DATEDIFF('minute', c.received_at, c.sent_at) AS routing_minutes
FROM fact_complaint c
JOIN dim_date d ON c.received_date_key = d.date_key
JOIN dim_product p ON c.product_key = p.product_key
JOIN dim_company co ON c.company_key = co.company_key;
